Since 1994

Vikram Varma & Associates was established as an independent boutique practice in 1994, developing special competence in sustainability, energy conservation and wellness through research-in-practice.

Philosophy

We do not propagate a fixed style or aesthetic. Each design is a process of discovery where solutions are found appropriate to the project at hand.

Work method

The studio works as a tight-knit team, bringing senior design thinking into every stage while maintaining precision and control across design and decision-making.

Belief

Architecture is treated as an evolving practice of consciousness, responsibility and resource awareness.
